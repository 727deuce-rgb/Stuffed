# OmniEcosystem product direction

OmniEcosystem is the new product identity for Stuffed: a family of useful digital products connected by a transparent, reviewable hub.

## Product family

- **OmniHub**: the discovery and account layer.
- **OmniStorage**: creator and project storage.
- **OmniGames**: games and digital worlds.
- **OmniCoin**: a proposed ecosystem utility concept, not sold or marketed as an investment.
- **OmniStable**: a research sandbox only. It does not issue a stablecoin, hold reserves, promise redemption, or process payments.

## Safe original-version boundary

The MVP can build product experiences, project documentation, simulations, and testnet demos. It must not accept user funds, custody private keys, sell tokens, promise appreciation, provide lending/yield, or imply that OmniStable is a live financial product.

Any future mainnet, payment, token distribution, reserve, redemption, or financial-product feature requires jurisdiction-specific legal advice, an appropriate operating structure, professional security review, and approved service providers before implementation.
