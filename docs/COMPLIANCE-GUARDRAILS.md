# Stuffed compliance and product guardrails

This document is a product-planning checklist, not legal advice. Counsel must review the exact launch jurisdictions and business model.

## MVP boundaries

- No custody or private-key storage.
- No lending, staking, yield, fiat redemption, exchange, or order book.
- No token price, profit, appreciation, scarcity, or liquidity claims.
- No automatic deployment: every transaction is explicitly prepared and user-signed.
- Testnet-only until security, legal, and operations sign-off.
- Token-name screening for vulgarity, hate, impersonation, fraud, and trademark abuse.

## Required review before mainnet

- Securities and investment-contract analysis for every token flow.
- FinCEN/MSB and state money-transmitter analysis.
- AML/KYC, sanctions, transaction monitoring, and suspicious-activity procedures where applicable.
- Consumer-protection, advertising, privacy, tax, and platform-policy review.
- Stablecoin/reserve analysis if StuffedCoin is marketed as redeemable or USD-backed.
- Smart-contract audit, admin-key review, incident response, and abuse reporting.

## AI outputs

The white-paper pillar and tokenomics pillar must label outputs as AI-generated drafts. They must not invent audits, reserves, partnerships, revenue, approvals, or guarantees. Human approval is required before publication.

## Fee policy

Any Stuffed service fee must be shown before confirmation, identify the recipient, state whether it applies to an app service or a third-party transaction, and be reviewed for licensing implications. Do not use a hidden transfer tax or undisclosed owner wallet skim.
