# What Stuffed sells

Stuffed sells **software access and creator workflow services**. It is not initially selling StuffedCoin, investment contracts, financial returns, custody, lending, or token liquidity.

## Customer problem

Token creators often have scattered notes, unclear supply models, hidden contract permissions, and weak risk disclosures. New users may not understand what they are signing or what a rug pull means.

## Stuffed promise

Stuffed helps a creator turn a project idea into a reviewable project brief, white-paper draft, tokenomics model, risk checklist, and testnet deployment plan.

## Customer receives

- A workspace for project briefs and versions
- AI-assisted white-paper drafts clearly labeled as drafts
- Supply, allocation, vesting, consumption, and burn scenarios
- Plain-language contract and rug-pull risk education
- Testnet deployment preparation where enabled
- Exportable documentation and hosted project pages on paid tiers

## What Stuffed does not promise

- Profit or token appreciation
- Legal or financial advice
- An audit or security certification
- Liquidity or an exchange listing
- A safe token merely because a project uses Stuffed
- A successful launch

## Why someone may pay

A creator can pay to save time, organize a serious project, communicate transparently with a community, collaborate with a team, and catch obvious risks before spending money on engineering or an audit. The buyer is paying for workflow and documentation—not for access to a guaranteed market.

## Initial monetization

- Free testnet workspace for adoption
- Monthly Builder and Studio subscriptions
- Fixed-price document exports or analysis runs
- Hosted documentation and collaboration features
- Later, disclosed referrals to independent auditors or service providers

Do not introduce transaction percentages, token taxes, custody, lending, yield, or a redeemable USD-backed token until the business model receives jurisdiction-specific legal review.
